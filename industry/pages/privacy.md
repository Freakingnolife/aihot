## What this notice covers

This notice covers the AdditiveOS News (AdditiveOS Radar) site (the news pages at additiveos.com) and the AdditiveOS waitlist (the signup form on the Advisor page). It does not cover the AdditiveOS application, which is not yet released.

## Reading the news

You can read the news without an account. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not build reading profiles. We count visits in aggregate, without cookies, using our own self-hosted Umami analytics, to see how many people read the site and where they came from. We do not track individuals, and we respect your browser's Do Not Track setting.

Bookmarks, read history and display preferences are stored only in your browser. Clearing your browser storage removes them. Bookmark files you export stay wherever you save them.

Our web server keeps standard request logs (such as IP address, time, page requested and browser type) to keep the site running and to diagnose failures. We keep these logs for up to 30 days.

The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes connection data, including your IP address, to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse.

## News content

Articles are short summaries written by AdditiveOS from publicly available publisher pages, with the publisher credited and linked. Publisher photos are shown with credit. If you are a publisher and want a summary or photo removed or corrected, contact us and we will act promptly.

## Feedback

If you use the feedback form, we receive your message, the page you were on, and, if you choose to add them, your email address and a screenshot. Please do not include passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information. We use feedback only to answer you and improve the site, and keep it for up to 12 months.

## The waitlist

When you join the waitlist, we store your email address, the time you signed up and gave consent, and your name and company if you choose to provide them. We use this only to tell you when AdditiveOS launches and to understand who is interested. We keep waitlist records until AdditiveOS launches and for up to 12 months afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

The signup database does not store your IP address, questions, CAD files or a browser fingerprint. Cloudflare Turnstile checks that signups come from a person: your browser connects to Cloudflare, and the signup service sends the verification token and your IP address to Cloudflare for that check only.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Service providers, such as our hosting, Cloudflare and email providers, process information only as needed to run the site and send the messages you asked for.

## Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or withdraw your waitlist consent, by emailing marcus@additiveos.com or replying to any waitlist email from the address you registered. We handle requests in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act.

Answer-first summary, adapted from the original AIHOT rules:
1. Begin with publisher attribution and the actual action: one self-contained sentence saying who did what and the key result or change. Answer the original title's core event, not a background detail. No lead-in phrases such as "This article discusses".
2. Keep the first sentence short, ideally under 30 words.
3. Then add only one or two points, ordered from verifiable fact or figure (with units and conditions) to what it means for additive-manufacturing users or the industry, or its availability. One point per sentence.
4. Write 2–3 compact sentences as one paragraph, at most 800 characters. No labels such as "Key points:", no bullets, no line breaks.
5. When the source does not support an impact, state only the facts. Answer-first is never a licence to add judgement the source does not support. Preserve qualifications. No Chinese translation.
